# Vulta

Vulta is a personal workspace for artists making their own music: a jumping off point and a homebase where you watch your progress come to life. This glossary is the walled garden. Everything in Vulta's world has exactly one name, and it lives here.

## Language

### Objects

**Idea**:
A captured spark of raw material: audio, words, an image, a link. Ideas are used, not cataloged: develop them into a Cut, drag them into the DAW as samples, or keep them as references. Using one changes nothing about it.
_Avoid_: memo, file, demo

**Cut**:
A developing song on its way to becoming a Record. (Name under review: "cut" carries music-industry placement slang.)
_Avoid_: project, song, track, workspace

**Record**:
A finished, mastered track. The anchor object of the garden: done, approved, real. A Record can carry its own Versions (remasters, alternate masters); an approved file is never silently replaced.
_Avoid_: release, workspace, project

**Album**:
An artist-declared collection of Records with its own identity and artwork. A Record can stand alone and appear on an Album at the same time.
_Avoid_: playlist, folder, EP

**Bounce**:
An exported audio file bounced out of a DAW Session. A Cut's Bounces stack by date into its version history, and the artist can mark one as approved.
_Avoid_: export, render, mix

**Version**:
A Bounce's place in a Cut's or Record's history. History stacks by date and you can roll back to any Version, like git. Rolling back changes what is current, never what is kept.
_Avoid_: revision, save, backup

**Session**:
A linked DAW project file. Opening a Session means opening the DAW. A Session needs no Cut: what it bounces out can land in the Dump as Ideas, and that is enough.
_Avoid_: project, DAW file, sitting

**Mark**:
A kept moment on an Idea, made in one gesture while listening, optionally carrying a few of the artist's words.
_Avoid_: tag, annotation, comment

**Moment**:
A celebration of real development, detected from history, never requested. Big Moments mark a Record's birth; small Moments mark work that moves an Idea or Cut to its next stage. Never awarded for mere activity.
_Avoid_: achievement, badge, streak

**Credit**:
A confirmed link between a person, a role, and a Record. Credits assemble themselves from provenance, who made what and who sent what, and the artist confirms them in small moments everywhere, never in one big form.
_Avoid_: metadata, form, claim

### Actions

**Dump**:
The act of getting anything into Vulta, available everywhere, never a place. What lands goes where it belongs: unattached audio becomes an Idea on the Canvas, a Bounce dropped on its Cut becomes a new Version. Nothing is required at capture: no naming, no sorting, no decisions.
_Avoid_: inbox, drop zone, import dialog

### Places

**Canvas**:
The spatial home of Ideas, where each Idea sits near what it sounds like. The place you wander your material.
_Avoid_: board, map, grid grid

**Discovery**:
The listening room. Your Records and Cuts next to the artists you admire, so you can hear the gap and get ideas.
_Avoid_: explore, references, browse

**Shelf**:
The home of finished work: your Records and Albums, always visible. The shelf grows, and that growth is the reward.
_Avoid_: library, collection, catalog
